# Abdullah Ahmadi

I use GIS and satellite data, such (Landsat, PlanetScope and SAR, etc.) to study land, coastal and mountain environments, cities, and renewable energy potential. I am based in Padova, Italy.

I have a background in geology and previously worked as an environmental geologist at Afghanistan's Ministry of Mines and Petroleum.
I completed my MSc in Environmental Sustainability at the University of Padova (UNIPD) in 2025 (110/110 cum laude).
For my thesis, carried out during a seven-month internship at the Department of Civil, Environmental and Architectural Engineering (DICEA), I used QGIS, GEE and R with LiDAR, vector and raster data to identify rooftops in Padova suitable for green roofs and solar PV. Suitable roofs were then prioritized for intervention by combining socio-economic and demographic indicators (e.g. age, economic status, migration background) with measures of environmental deficit (e.g. NDVI, NDBI, UHI), so that interventions could target the areas with the greatest need (climate justice). This work was later published in Urban Climate (2026). After graduating, I joined the same department as a research fellow for six months, working on spatial analysis and multi-criteria methods for urban greenery, urban agroecology, climate hazards, climate adaptation and climate justice-related projects.

## Work

**Rooftops for solar PV and green roofs in Padova**
I mapped which roofs in Padova are suitable for photovoltaics, green roofs (intensive, extensive), rooftop agroecology, or all, using a GIS-MCDA approach. The study found about 11.67 km² of suitable roof area on 29,493 buildings, with a PV potential of around 0.837 TWh per year. Published in *Urban Climate* (2026), first author. [Read the paper]( https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2212095526000714)
